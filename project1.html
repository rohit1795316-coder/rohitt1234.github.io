To bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern technology, increase crop productivity, reduce post-harvest losses, and ensure fair prices for farmers.

Government Schemes

Access Schemes & Benefits Available for Farmers

PM Kisan

Direct financial support of ₹6000 per year to small and marginal farmers to help them meet expenses related to agriculture.

Crop Insurance

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana provides insurance coverage against crop failures and natural disasters.

Kisan Credit Card

Easy access to credit at competitive interest rates for purchasing seeds, fertilizers, and farming equipment.

Soil Health Card

Comprehensive soil analysis and personalized recommendations for improving soil health and crop productivity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Portal

How do I register on the portal?

▼

Registration is simple and free. Click on "Register Now", fill in your basic details including name, contact number, and farming information, and you'll have instant access to all our services.

What information can I get about market prices?

▼

Our portal provides real-time market prices for major crops, updated hourly from various mandis across India. You can track price trends and make informed selling decisions.

How can I avail government schemes through this portal?

▼

We provide complete information and links to apply for various government schemes like PM Kisan, crop insurance, and Kisan Credit Card. Simply navigate to the Schemes section and follow the application guidelines.

Is there a consultation fee for AI advisory?

▼

No, our AI advisory service is completely free for all registered users. You can get instant responses to your farming queries 24/7.

Can I get soil testing done through the portal?

▼

Yes, you can schedule soil testing through our portal. We'll arrange for sample collection and provide detailed analysis with personalized recommendations.
